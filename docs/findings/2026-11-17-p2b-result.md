# 2026-11-17: P2B result, NBM snowfall calibration against CLI (no prices)

Registered in `docs/preregistration.md` (P2B), frozen 2026-11-17 04:56 UTC at

**Result: no documented NBM weakness.** A logistic recalibration of NBM's
snowfall probabilities, fitted on winters 2021-22 to 2024-25, did not improve the
held-out winter 2025-26: dB = Brier(raw) − Brier(recalibrated) = −0.0001, 95% CI
[−0.0017, +0.0014] (186,914 case-thresholds, 23 weeks). NBM has clear patterns in
past winters, but they change from winter to winter and version to version, so a
fixed correction learned from the past does not hold.

## Sample

13 snow settlement stations (Chicago Midway reports no snowfall, so its windows
drop), November to March, winters 2021-22 to 2025-26. Each case: one window of 1,
2 or 3 climate days, forecast 1, 2, 3, 5 or 7 days before it starts from the NBM
cycles public at that time, mapped onto climate days with the P2A composite.
133,665 cases (10,284 per station), each scored at 7 thresholds (above 0.1, 1, 2,
4, 6, 8, 12 inches). Leakage counts 0 / 0.

## Development winters (descriptive)

- **Skill falls with lead.** For measurable snow (above 0.1 in), skill against
  climatology is 0.43 at 1 day, 0.36 at 2, 0.26 at 3, 0.12 at 5 and about 0 at 7.
  For 1 and 2 inches the pattern is the same (0.45 and 0.41 at 1 day, near 0 by
  7 days).
- **Too much measurable snow, growing with lead.** Mean probability 15% against
  12% observed at 1 day, 18% against 13% at 5 days.
- **Somewhat overconfident for 1 to 2 inches** (recalibration slopes 0.73 to
  0.82); close to calibrated at 4 and 6 inches (0.85 to 1.04).
- **Unstable across NBM versions and winters.** V4.0 (2021-22 to early 2023)
  forecast measurable snow 21% of the time against 12% observed; V4.1 13% against
  11%. Large thresholds are dominated by which winters had big storms: V4.2's
  development span had no 8-inch event at all, so its skill there is meaningless.
  Version and winter cannot be separated in this sample: V4.0 ran 2021-11 to
  2023-01-17, V4.1 to the end of 2023-24, V4.2 was the 2024-25 winter, and the
  held-out winter is entirely V4.3, a version the correction never saw.

## Reading

- NBM is a strong baseline at 1 to 3 days and its probabilities are roughly
  calibrated for the totals Kalshi trades (1 to 8 inches). There is no stable
  bias to correct.
- P2A found the Kalshi market more accurate than this same composite. Together
  with P2B, the edge cannot come from fixing NBM's calibration. A snow model has
  to add information NBM does not carry when the market prices: observed
  snowfall during the storm, hourly convection-allowing guidance (HRRR), the
  snow-to-liquid ratio, timing within the climate day.
- That points to the storm-window nowcast in the Phase 4 queue, tested
  prospectively (P2A-S), not to a recalibrated NBM.

## Decision (D-047)

No documented NBM weakness. NBM enters the snow model as an input, unrecalibrated.
Next for snow: write a plan (a storm-window nowcast built on
in-storm observations and HRRR) before building anything.
