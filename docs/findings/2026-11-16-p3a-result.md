# 2026-11-16: P3A result. The Kalshi ERCOT market beat ERCOT's own load forecast

Experiment P3A, pre-registered in `docs/preregistration.md` (frozen 2026-11-16
02:45 UTC, one deviation logged before scoring). Run once, 2026-11-16 05:08 UTC,
with `uv run pmw p3a`. Full tables: [`p3a_result_tables.md`](p3a_result_tables.md).

## Verdict

**CONDITIONAL under the registered rule.** The accuracy result is clear and points
against us: the market's probabilities were *more* accurate than probabilities
built from ERCOT's own published load forecast. It is not a NO GO only because
the small simulated taker strategy happened to make money (+$261 on 2,249
contracts, positive in 94% of resamples; NO GO needed under 50%). That P&L is
not evidence of edge: the bigger the disagreement with the market, the *worse*
the trade did (correlation -0.33).

## What was compared

For every settled KXTXERCOTPEAKD market with operating day 2026-08-28 to
2026-11-14 (all 1,334 reproduce from ERCOT data, D-036), at 00:00, 06:00, 10:00
and 14:00 Central on the operating day, two probabilities that the day's
first-complete NP6-346-CD peak is above the strike:

- **Market:** mid of the hourly candle ending at the decision time, two-sided,
  spread at most 0.20.
- **ERCOT forecast:** the latest seven-day load forecast (NP3-565-CD) posted
  before the decision time, in-use model hour by hour, daily maximum F; P = share
  of historical error ratios A/F (same posting slot, same season, 2017-07 to
  2026-08-27, before any Kalshi market) for which F x ratio exceeds the strike.

1,551 observations, 785 markets, 76 operating days, 12 calendar weeks.
Leakage check: 0 postings at or after the decision time; every training day
before the test period.

## Results

| | D = Brier(market) - Brier(ERCOT) | 95% CI |
| - | -: | - |
| **Primary** | **-0.0110** | **[-0.0178, -0.0035]** |
| Spread <= 0.10 | -0.0066 | [-0.0118, +0.0012] |
| Spread <= 0.30 | -0.0145 | [-0.0210, -0.0077] |
| Training on the last 3 years only | -0.0102 | [-0.0181, -0.0013] |

Negative D means the market was more accurate. Every registered variant has the
same sign.

| Forecast | Brier | Log loss | Reliability | Resolution |
| - | -: | -: | -: | -: |
| Kalshi market | **0.056** | **0.208** | 0.0021 | 0.191 |
| ERCOT forecast | 0.067 | 0.229 | 0.0027 | 0.180 |

Both are well calibrated; the market separates outcomes better (higher resolution).

**By decision time** the market is ahead at every hour: 00 CT -0.014, 06 CT
-0.007, 10 CT -0.011, 14 CT -0.018. At 17:00 CT (secondary, 74 observations) it
is far ahead (-0.19): by late afternoon the market sees the day's real-time load,
which a forecast posted at 16:30 does not contain.

**Hottest days** (top 20% of peaks, 247 observations, 5 weeks): D = -0.005,
interval [-0.040, +0.015]. No evidence either way where it would matter most.

**Taker** (registered naive rule): tau 0.05 +$156 (81% of resamples positive),
tau 0.10 +$261 (94%), tau 0.15 +$75 (67%). 40 to 62 trades in total; fees
$13 to $19. Perceived-versus-realized edge correlation is negative at every
threshold (-0.19 to -0.33).

**Capacity** (Phase 3 gate): the median stressed day traded 37,855 contracts
(about $16,800 of premium); gate (20,000) met. The median day overall traded
18,568. The 16 stressed days all fall between Aug 28 and Sep 22: capacity is a
summer phenomenon, and November days trade a few hundred contracts.

## What this means

1. **ERCOT's free forecast is priced in.** Same as snow with NBM (P2A): the
   public forecast, used straightforwardly, does not beat the Kalshi market, and
   the gap is statistically clear.
2. **The market uses information the day-ahead forecast lacks**, most visibly
   real-time load in the afternoon. Any ERCOT model would have to beat the market
   *intraday*, against traders watching ERCOT's live dashboard.
3. **Per the registered rule (CONDITIONAL):** no ERCOT model work (Phase 4)
   unless a specific, documented forecast weakness turns up; a prospective test
   on live-logged order books through summer 2027 (P3A-S, to be registered
   separately) is the next ERCOT step. Capacity is not the obstacle in summer.
4. **A lead, not a finding:** between 0.15 and 0.45 the ERCOT-based
   probabilities came true less often than stated (0.15 -> 0.08, 0.35 -> 0.27,
   0.46 -> 0.40), consistent with ERCOT's forecasts running high this autumn
   relative to the 2017-2026 error history. Worth a look in a separately
   registered study; not a reason to trade.

## Limits of this result

- 12 calendar weeks. With so few blocks, percentile intervals tend to be too
  narrow; the primary interval excludes zero comfortably, but the hottest-days
  stratum (5 weeks) is uninformative.
- Hourly candles, not order books: the mid can be stale or thin. Quotes are
  nearly empty right after the 23:00 CT open (148 usable observations at 00 CT).
- The ERCOT probability is one registered construction (empirical error ratios
  by slot and season). A model using weather forecasts, real-time load or
  zone-level structure could do better; that is a Phase 4 question the rule now
  gates.
- The sample is late summer into autumn of one year. Winter cold snaps, the
  other stressed regime, are not in it.
