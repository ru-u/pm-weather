So the Kalshi side of the Phase 2 comparison is roughly 60 city-months plus 6
storm windows, and those are far from independent (one storm moves four
cities; one month's strikes move together). The honest count of independent
events is in the low tens. This limits what Phase 2 can prove and is built
into the pre-registration draft.

Volume, on the other hand, was real last winter: storm-window markets alone
traded over 10 million contracts across six events.

## 5. NBM guidance is easier to get than expected

Station text bulletins are archived per cycle on AWS back to at least January
2021, and the probabilistic bulletin (NBP) carries 24-hour snowfall percentiles
(10/25/50/75/90) out to about 9.5 days for each station. The version number is
in every header. Phase 2 can start from text, with no GRIB processing.

Caveat found already: NBP's 24-hour periods end at 12Z, which is not a climate
day. The January 25, 2026 bulletin was labelled V4.3, so "NBM v5" does not
cover last winter's Kalshi markets.
